A good white T-shirt costs little, goes with almost everything and looks right in every season. The trick is in the fit, the fabric and what you pair it with. Here are seven outfits that work every time.

## First, choose the right tee

- **Fabric:** look for a mid-weight cotton (roughly 160–200 gsm if the label says). Thin cotton turns see-through and twists after a few washes.
- **Neckline:** a crew neck is the most versatile. A slightly wider or lower neck is softer and more flattering if you have a short neck or a larger bust.
- **Fit:** the shoulder seam should sit on your shoulder bone, or just past it for a relaxed fit. If it's tight across the chest or the sleeves cut in, size up.
- **Length:** hip-length tucks in neatly and still looks finished worn loose.

## 1. Straight-leg jeans and loafers

The classic. Do a half-tuck at the front to show your waist, add a belt, and finish with loafers or clean white trainers. Blue denim, white tee and one gold piece of jewellery is a formula you can repeat all year. Our [white trainers guide](/white-trainers-guide/) covers the shoes that go with it.

## 2. Under a blazer for the office

A crisp white tee makes a tailored blazer look modern rather than stiff. Keep everything else simple: tailored trousers or dark jeans, and a pointed flat or a low block heel. Choose a heavier tee here so it holds its shape under the jacket.

## 3. With a midi skirt

A white tee tucked into a satin or pleated midi skirt is an easy way to dress a skirt down for daytime. Add trainers for the weekend or ankle boots in autumn.

## 4. Layered under a slip dress

When it's too cool for a slip dress on its own, wear a fitted white tee underneath. It adds coverage and warmth, and makes an evening dress work in daylight.

## 5. All-neutral with wide-leg trousers

Pair the tee with cream, beige or grey wide-leg trousers and a trench or long cardigan. Tonal outfits like this look polished with almost no effort, and every piece mixes with everything else you own.

## 6. With a knit over the shoulders

On a mild day, throw a jumper over your shoulders, or tie it loosely at the front. It adds colour and texture without the bulk, and you have a layer ready when the weather turns.

## 7. Dressed up with tailoring and heels

A good white tee, high-waisted tailored trousers and heeled mules or slingbacks is a smart-casual outfit for dinner. Add a structured bag and statement earrings and the tee reads as a deliberate choice.

## How to keep it white

- Wash whites together at 30–40°C and avoid overloading the machine.
- Treat deodorant marks and collar stains before washing.
- Dry in the shade, or inside out, to stop yellowing.
- Replace tees once the neckline stretches. A tired tee makes the whole outfit look tired.

## The takeaway

Buy one well-fitting, good-weight white tee and you have the base for dozens of outfits. It's the first piece on our [capsule wardrobe checklist](/capsule-wardrobe-checklist/) for a reason. Start with jeans and a blazer, then try it with the skirts and trousers you already own.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I wear with a white T-shirt?

Almost anything. The most reliable pairings are straight-leg jeans, tailored trousers, midi skirts and wide-leg trousers, finished with loafers, white trainers or ankle boots.

### How do I stop a white T-shirt from going see-through?

Choose a mid-weight cotton, roughly 160–200 gsm, and wear a skin-tone bra or vest underneath rather than a white one.

### Should a white T-shirt be tucked in?

A half-tuck or full tuck defines your waist and looks more polished with high-waisted trousers and skirts. Leave it loose with relaxed jeans or under a blazer for a casual look.

### How do I keep white T-shirts white?

Wash whites together at 30–40°C, treat stains before washing, avoid overloading the machine and dry them out of direct sun to stop yellowing.
