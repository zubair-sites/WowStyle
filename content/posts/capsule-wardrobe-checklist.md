A capsule wardrobe is a small set of pieces that all work together. You own less, but every item earns its place, and getting dressed takes minutes rather than half an hour.

This checklist is a starting point, not a rulebook. Adjust it for your lifestyle: if you work from home, swap some tailoring for knitwear; if you're in an office, do the reverse.

## Before you start

1. **Empty your wardrobe** (or one section at a time) and sort into *love*, *maybe* and *don't wear*.
2. **Choose a base palette.** Pick two or three neutrals (for example navy, cream and camel, or black, grey and white) plus one or two accent colours you love wearing.
3. **Note the gaps** as you go through the list below. Those are the only things you need to buy.

## Tops (8)

- [ ] 2 white or cream T-shirts (see [how to style a white T-shirt](/how-to-style-a-white-t-shirt/))
- [ ] 1 black or navy T-shirt
- [ ] 1 striped long-sleeve top
- [ ] 1 crisp shirt (white or pale blue)
- [ ] 1 silky or dressier blouse
- [ ] 2 fine-knit jumpers (crew or V-neck)

## Bottoms (5)

- [ ] 1 pair of straight or slim dark jeans
- [ ] 1 pair of lighter or wide-leg jeans
- [ ] 1 pair of tailored trousers
- [ ] 1 midi skirt
- [ ] 1 pair of relaxed trousers or joggers you'd actually leave the house in

## Dresses and layers (6)

- [ ] 1 versatile day dress
- [ ] 1 dress you can wear in the evening
- [ ] 1 chunky cardigan or knit
- [ ] 1 blazer
- [ ] 1 trench or light raincoat (essential for a UK spring and autumn)
- [ ] 1 warm wool or wool-blend coat

## Shoes (5)

- [ ] 1 pair of clean white trainers (our [white trainers guide](/white-trainers-guide/))
- [ ] 1 pair of loafers or smart flats
- [ ] 1 pair of ankle boots
- [ ] 1 pair of heels or heeled mules
- [ ] 1 pair of sandals

## Accessories (6)

- [ ] 1 everyday tote or shoulder bag (how to [choose a handbag that lasts](/how-to-choose-a-handbag/))
- [ ] 1 smaller bag for evenings
- [ ] 1 leather belt
- [ ] 1 pair of sunglasses
- [ ] 1 scarf (wool for winter, silk or cotton for the rest of the year)
- [ ] A few pieces of everyday jewellery

## How to make it work

- **Check every new piece against three others.** If you can't make three outfits with things you already own, don't buy it.
- **Fit matters more than price.** A tailor can take up hems and nip in waists for less than you'd expect.
- **Rotate by season.** Store heavy knits and coats in summer and sandals in winter, so your wardrobe only shows what you can wear now.
- **Review twice a year.** In spring and autumn, look at what you didn't wear and ask why.

## The takeaway

You don't need all 30 pieces straight away. Start with what you own, fill the biggest gaps first (usually a good coat, good jeans and comfortable shoes), and build slowly.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many items should a capsule wardrobe have?

Most capsule wardrobes have 30 to 40 pieces per season, including shoes and outerwear. The exact number matters less than every piece working with several others.

### What colours work best for a capsule wardrobe?

Pick two or three neutrals, such as navy, cream and camel or black, grey and white, plus one or two accent colours you love. Everything should mix with everything else.

### Do I have to throw clothes away to start a capsule wardrobe?

No. Start with what you own, store or donate what you never wear, and only buy to fill the biggest gaps, usually a good coat, good jeans and comfortable shoes.

### How often should I update a capsule wardrobe?

Review it twice a year, in spring and autumn. Swap seasonal pieces in and out and note what you didn't wear and why.
