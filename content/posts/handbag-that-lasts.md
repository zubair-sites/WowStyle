Your everyday bag goes everywhere with you, so it's worth choosing carefully. A well-made bag in a classic shape will outlast several trend pieces and work with everything you own.

## Start with how you live

Before you look at styles, empty your current bag and look at what you carry every day. Then ask:

- Do you need room for a laptop or tablet?
- Do you walk or commute a lot? (A crossbody or shoulder strap will be more comfortable.)
- Do you need to find things quickly? (Look for an inside pocket or two.)

## Choose a shape

- **Tote:** roomy and practical for work and travel. Look for a zip or magnetic closure.
- **Shoulder bag:** smart and easy, the most versatile everyday choice.
- **Crossbody:** hands-free and secure, ideal for city days and travel.
- **Top-handle bag:** more structured and polished, good for work and occasions.

## Check the quality

- **Leather:** full-grain or top-grain leather ages well and develops character. Run your hand over it: it should feel supple, not plasticky. If you prefer vegan options, look for thicker, well-finished materials with neat edges.
- **Stitching:** small, even and tight, with no loose threads, especially around the handles.
- **Edges:** look for smoothly painted or folded edges, not raw edges that will fray.
- **Hardware:** zips should run smoothly, and buckles and clasps should feel heavy and solid.
- **Lining:** a sturdy lining (cotton, canvas or suede) lasts longer than thin polyester.

## Pick a colour you'll actually use

For your first good bag, choose a neutral that suits your wardrobe: black, tan, chocolate brown, navy or taupe. Tan and brown are often easier to wear than black and look softer with denim.

## Look after it

- Store it stuffed with paper, in its dust bag, away from direct sunlight.
- Don't hang heavy bags by their handles for long periods.
- Treat leather with a conditioner once or twice a year.
- Wipe up spills straight away, and have a cobbler or bag repairer fix worn handles early.

## The takeaway

Choose a shape that suits your daily life, check the leather, stitching and hardware, and pick a neutral colour. Look after it and it will last for years.
