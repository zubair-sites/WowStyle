Autumn in the UK can mean three seasons in one day: a cold morning, a mild afternoon and a shower on the way home. The answer is layering, but done well, so you stay comfortable without looking like you're wearing everything you own.

## The three rules of layering

1. **Thin to thick.** Start with the thinnest layer next to your skin and finish with the heaviest. Fine knits, shirts and long-sleeve tees make the best base layers.
2. **Vary the lengths.** Let a shirt hem show below a jumper, or a long coat sit over a shorter jacket. Different lengths look intentional.
3. **Mix textures, not too many colours.** Knit, wool, leather and cotton together look rich. Keep colours to two or three so the outfit doesn't look busy.

## Five autumn outfit formulas

### 1. Trench, knit and jeans
A fine-knit jumper, straight jeans and a trench coat, with loafers or ankle boots. If it's warm by lunchtime, the trench comes off and the outfit still works.

### 2. Shirt under a jumper
Wear a crisp shirt under a crew-neck jumper, with the collar and cuffs showing. Add tailored trousers for work or jeans for the weekend.

### 3. Midi skirt and knee boots
A midi skirt, a fitted knit and knee-high boots keep you warm without tights in early autumn. Add a blazer or coat as the temperature drops.

### 4. The leather jacket layer
A leather (or faux leather) jacket over a hoodie or knit, with jeans and boots. Add a scarf and you're ready for colder evenings.

### 5. Tonal neutrals
Camel coat, cream knit, beige trousers. Dressing in shades of one colour looks polished, and every piece mixes with the others.

## Autumn essentials worth having

- A water-resistant trench or mac
- Two or three fine-knit jumpers (merino is warm but thin)
- Ankle boots you can walk in all day
- A large wool scarf that doubles as a wrap
- A crossbody bag, so your hands are free for an umbrella

## The takeaway

Build outfits from light layers you can take off rather than one heavy one. Keep the palette simple and the textures interesting, and you'll be ready for whatever the day brings.
