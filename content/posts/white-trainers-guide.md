Few things work as hard as a pair of clean white trainers. They make jeans look relaxed, dresses look modern and tailoring look effortless. Here's how to choose a pair that you'll wear for years.

## What to look for

- **A low, simple profile.** Minimal leather trainers with a slim sole look smarter and go with more than chunky or sporty styles.
- **Leather or good-quality vegan leather.** It's easier to wipe clean than canvas and holds its shape better.
- **A small or tonal logo.** The quieter the branding, the more outfits they'll suit.
- **Comfort first.** Try them on in the afternoon, when your feet are slightly larger, and walk around for a few minutes. Look for a removable insole if you need extra support.

## How to wear them

### With jeans
The easiest outfit there is. Straight or slim jeans cropped at the ankle show off the shoe; wide-leg jeans should just touch the top of the trainer.

### With dresses and skirts
A white trainer with a midi dress is a weekend favourite, and they work just as well with [a white T-shirt and jeans](/how-to-style-a-white-t-shirt/). It works with florals, knitted dresses and satin slip skirts alike.

### With tailoring
Pair them with a relaxed suit or tailored trousers for a smart-casual office. Keep the trainers spotless here; scuffs are noticeable next to sharp tailoring.

### With all-neutral outfits
White trainers finish a cream, beige or grey outfit neatly, and they lighten an all-black look.

## How to keep them white

1. **Protect them** with a stain and water-repellent spray before you first wear them.
2. **Wipe them weekly** with a damp cloth and a little washing-up liquid.
3. **Clean the soles** with an old toothbrush and a paste of bicarbonate of soda and water.
4. **Wash the laces separately**, or simply replace them. New laces make old trainers look new.
5. **Stuff them with paper** when they're wet and let them dry naturally, away from radiators.

## The takeaway

Choose a simple, low-profile leather pair that's comfortable from day one, then look after it. A good pair of white trainers will see you through every season.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do white trainers go with?

Straight or slim jeans, midi dresses and skirts, relaxed suits and tailored trousers, and all-neutral outfits. A simple, low-profile pair works with almost everything.

### Can you wear white trainers with a dress?

Yes. White trainers with a midi dress are a classic weekend look and work with florals, knitted dresses and satin slip skirts.

### How do I clean white trainers?

Wipe them weekly with a damp cloth and a little washing-up liquid, scrub the soles with bicarbonate of soda paste, wash or replace the laces, and let them dry naturally away from radiators.

### Are leather or canvas white trainers better?

Leather or good vegan leather is easier to wipe clean and holds its shape better. Canvas is lighter and cheaper but marks more easily.
