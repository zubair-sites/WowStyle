*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

The Wow Style is free to read. To keep it that way, some of our articles may contain **affiliate links**. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission from the retailer. It doesn't cost you anything extra.

## Our promise to you

- **We only recommend things we believe are worth it.** Commission never decides what we write about or what we say.
- **We label it.** Posts that contain affiliate links say so near the top, and affiliate links are marked.
- **Sponsored content is always clearly marked.** If a brand pays for a post or gives us a product to review, we'll label it *#ad*, *sponsored* or *gifted*, in line with the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and the CAP Code.
- **Brands don't approve our content.** We don't let brands review or change our editorial articles before we publish them.
