*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

This policy explains what personal data The Wow Style ("we", "us") collects when you use thewowstyle.co.uk, why we collect it and what your rights are under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

## Who we are

The Wow Style is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can contact us about privacy at any time through our [contact page](/contact/).

## What we collect and why

### When you contact us
If you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply to you. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry. We keep messages for up to 12 months unless an ongoing conversation needs them for longer.

### When you leave a comment
If you comment on a post, we collect the name, email address and website you enter, your comment, your IP address and your browser's user agent, to help prevent spam. Your comment and name are shown publicly; your email address is not. Comments may be checked by an automated spam-detection service. An anonymised string created from your email address may be sent to the Gravatar service to show your profile picture, if you have one.

### When you subscribe to our newsletter
If you sign up for emails, we collect your email address (and your name, if you give it) to send you the newsletter. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email. Our newsletter is sent using Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.), which stores subscriber data on our behalf and may transfer it to the USA under appropriate safeguards.

### When you browse the site
Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, pages visited and browser type) for security and to keep the site running. These are kept for a short period and are not used to identify you.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use:

- **Essential cookies** that make the site work, for example to remember your comment details if you tick the box to do so, or to keep logged-in editors signed in. These don't need your consent.
- **Affiliate cookies:** if you click an affiliate link and visit a retailer's site, the retailer or affiliate network may set a cookie to record that you came from us. This happens on their site and is covered by their privacy policy.

We don't currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add them, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working.

## Embedded content

Some posts may include embedded content (for example videos or social media posts). Embedded content behaves as if you had visited the other website, which may collect data about you and set cookies.

## Who we share data with

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site (such as our web host and email provider), and only as needed, or where the law requires it.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us through our [contact page](/contact/). We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
