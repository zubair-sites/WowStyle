*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

Our readers trust us to tell them what's worth wearing and buying. This page explains how we earn and keep that trust.

## Who we write for

We write for people in the UK who want to dress well for real life, on any budget, without chasing every trend.

## How we create articles

- **Useful first.** Every article starts with a real question readers ask, and aims to answer it clearly and completely.
- **Researched.** We draw on hands-on experience, garment and care labels, brand information, industry standards and reliable sources, and we link to sources where it helps.
- **Edited.** Every article is reviewed by an editor for accuracy, clarity and usefulness before it's published.
- **Kept up to date.** We review our guides regularly and update them when advice, products or standards change. The date on each article shows when it was published or last updated.

## Independence

Our editorial content is independent. Brands, retailers and advertisers can't pay to change our opinion, and they don't review articles before we publish them.

## Affiliate links and sponsored content

Some articles contain affiliate links, which may earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Commission never decides what we recommend. Sponsored articles, paid links and gifted products are always clearly labelled, and paid links carry `rel="sponsored"`. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for details.

## Guest contributors

Guest articles meet the same standards as our own and are edited by our team. See our [Write for Us](/write-for-us/) guidelines.

## Corrections

If you spot a mistake, please tell us through our [contact page](/contact/). We'll check it and correct the article promptly, and note significant corrections at the end of the article.
