The Wow Style is a UK fashion journal about getting dressed with confidence. We publish practical, honest articles that help readers choose, wear and look after their clothes. If you have expert knowledge or a fresh angle, we'd like to hear from you.

## What we publish

- **Outfit ideas:** outfit formulas for work, weekends, seasons and occasions.
- **Wardrobe essentials:** how to choose, buy and care for key pieces.
- **Accessories:** bags, shoes, sunglasses and jewellery.
- **Style guides:** fit, proportion, colour, body shape and dressing for your lifestyle.
- **Sustainable and considered fashion:** buying less, buying better, care and repair.

We don't publish news, celebrity gossip or pieces that only promote a product.

## Our standards

- **Original and exclusive.** Your article must not be published anywhere else, before or after.
- **Genuinely useful.** Answer a real question, with specific, practical advice readers can act on.
- **Well written.** Usually 1,000–2,000 words, in British English, with clear headings and short paragraphs.
- **Accurate.** Back up facts and claims, and link to reliable sources where it helps the reader.
- **Your own work.** We check every submission. Articles that are copied or spun, or written by AI without real expertise and editing, will be declined.

We edit every article for clarity, accuracy and house style, and we may change headlines and images.

## Links

- Up to two relevant links in the body of the article, plus one in the author bio.
- Links must point to useful, relevant pages, not to unrelated or low-quality sites.
- **Sponsored articles and paid links are always labelled** as sponsored, and their links carry `rel="sponsored"`, in line with Google's guidelines and the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) rules.

## What we don't accept

Gambling, adult content, CBD and vaping, crypto and trading, payday loans, weapons, or anything illegal, misleading or harmful.

## Sponsored articles

Brands and agencies can commission a sponsored article or sponsor an existing guide. Sponsored content follows the same quality standards as our own articles and is always clearly labelled. Contact us for rates and availability.

## How to pitch

Send your pitch through our [contact page](/contact/) and include:

1. Your proposed headline and a short outline (3–5 bullet points).
2. Why you're the right person to write it.
3. Links to two published writing samples.
4. Whether it's a guest post or a sponsored article.

We reply to every pitch within five working days. Please don't send a finished article until we've agreed the topic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you accept guest posts?

Yes. We accept original guest posts on fashion, style and wardrobe topics that meet our editorial standards. Pitch your idea through our contact page first.

### Do you charge for guest posts?

Editorial guest posts chosen on merit are free. Sponsored articles and posts that include commercial links are paid and always labelled as sponsored.

### Are links in guest posts dofollow?

Editorial links to genuinely useful sources are normal links. Links in sponsored or paid content carry rel="sponsored", as Google requires.

### How long does it take to publish?

Once the topic is agreed and the article is approved, we usually publish within two to three weeks.
